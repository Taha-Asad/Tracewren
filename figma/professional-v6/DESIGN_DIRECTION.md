# V6 / product refinement · 21 October 2026

The user’s latest direction is to make the product professional and authentic. This supersedes the earlier editorial treatment of the concept. The report workflows, actors, evidence references and verification outcomes are retained.

## What changed

- IBM Plex Sans replaces the oversized serif hierarchy. Report titles are 28px; major page titles are 26px; sections use 16–18px; report text is 14px with 20–22px leading. HTTP excerpts use IBM Plex Mono at 13px.
- White work surfaces and a restrained neutral sidebar replace the cream cast. Color distinguishes actions and meaningful states. A pending retest uses amber; a failed observation uses red.
- The report opens with its identity, asset, available action and current status. Large introductory copy and the repeated summary slogan are removed.
- Selectable request/response excerpts replace miniature browser windows and symbolic illustrations. Unnecessary verification and identity artwork is removed.
- Queue rows and navigation are more compact while keeping clear hit areas. Decorative stage numbering and duplicate workspace links are removed.
- Submission validation retains the report title, asset and current attachments. The message sits with the affected task. Other recovery messages use a focused reading width and content-sized actions.
- Prototype recovery catalogs remain in the review entry. Exhibition labels and generic recovery-state links are hidden in normal product screens.

Primary desktop, reference-sized, compact and mobile dimensions remain 1600 × 1080, 1488 × 1058, 1366 × 900 and 390 × 844 respectively. Existing report-column widths and 32px desktop insets remain; density comes from typography and removing decoration rather than crowding controls.

## Evidence and workflow integrity

The controlled-account report fixture remains consistent: BBT-0241, defender assessment 6.5 Medium, researcher suggestion 8.1 High. Attempt 1 uses Production v0.8.4; the replacement uses v0.8.5. Failure records, internal verification, researcher verification and timeout closure remain distinct.

New excerpts are readable source fixtures, not claims of downloaded attachment bytes. The native design does not execute account services, cryptography, signatures, downloads or backend authorization.
