Use case: logo-brand.
Create a finished, beautifully crafted primary logo for "Tracewren", a confidential vulnerability reporting and remediation workspace, part of a personal bird-themed brand called The Foolish Crow.
Asset: one clean horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, no presentation board or scene.
On the left, design a distinctive small wren in profile facing right. Give it the characteristic alert upturned tail, compact round breast, tiny pointed beak. The bird must be refined and abstract, built from just a few confident smooth filled shapes; recognizable silhouette, not a detailed illustration. Integrate ONE elegantly cut negative-space line through the wing that suggests a traced path, ending in a small circular terminal. It must feel like a natural feather detail first and a trace second. Avoid circuit-board clutter. Strong small-size legibility. Balanced visual weight with the wordmark.
To the right, exact text "Tracewren" (T-r-a-c-e-w-r-e-n), title case, one word, rendered in a beautifully spaced humanist sans-serif close to IBM Plex Sans SemiBold. Single graphite color #24292F for the whole word. No tagline, no trademark or other text.
Bird color solid muted deep forest green #2B5147. Trace cutout transparent. Flat colors with sharp clean edges. Spacious clear padding, horizontal composition, centered lockup. The result should feel mature, quiet, precise, original and carefully optically balanced, appropriate for a real professional security product with white and very light gray UI.
Avoid: bug imagery, shields, padlocks, generic swooshes, owls, eagles, aggressive gaming birds, origami, illustration hatching, thin fragile lines, gradients, glow, shadows, 3D, texture, mockups, frames, extra text, recoloring the word into multiple colors. Output only the isolated logo.
