# Design lessons · BugBountyTrack

The approved direction is the original professional graphite/ivory/petrol palette. Richer content and connected workflows from the second edition remain useful.

Visual tone comes from typography, shape, spacing, emphasis and wording as well as color. Preserve the approved palette while refining those presentation choices. Do not make a security workspace feel like a personal home dashboard through large warm banners, cream actions and rounded headings.

Use actual work material—reproduction, evidence, assignment, deadlines and next decisions—to create interest. Keep task-specific document/ledger layouts; avoid filling the product with repeated empty cards or invented metrics.

The native `check_palette.py` compares saved color values to the original token file. Run it with geometry/contrast/link checks after future refinements unless a new palette is explicitly approved. A passing structural check does not replace inspecting rendered work tasks.
